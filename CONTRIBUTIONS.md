# Contribution and attribution

## Designer / project owner

Game direction; requirements; selection and evaluation of layout changes; balance decisions; manual playtesting; bug reports; prioritization; acceptance of iteration results.

## AI assistance

ChatGPT/Codex assisted with technical implementation, C++, tooling, investigation, documentation and this English portfolio. The owner directs and evaluates that work. The portfolio does not claim sole hand-authorship of all code or professional studio production experience.

## References and assets

Dungeon Defenders and Dungeon Defenders II informed discussion of active hero combat, lane defense and progression. This portfolio contains no extracted assets or screenshots from those games and claims no affiliation.

The game uses Unreal Engine and prototype geometry. Any future screenshots, character models, sounds or downloadable builds require attribution appropriate to the assets actually included. No blanket open-source or asset redistribution license is asserted by this repository.

## Language

Original design discussions and much of the working documentation are Russian. Portfolio text is an English adaptation intended to preserve reasoning and verification limits. Historical numbers remain historical rather than being silently rewritten to match the current build.
