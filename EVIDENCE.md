## Evidence vocabulary

- **Documented implementation:** described in a retained implementation record.
- **Build verified:** compilation succeeded for the stated version; no gameplay conclusion follows automatically.
- **Static visual check:** a particular view was inspected.
- **Owner playtest report:** observed and reported by the designer; context may be incomplete.
- **Pending verification:** a scenario is defined, but no matching result is presented.

No new engine tests or gameplay sessions were run to assemble this portfolio. Historical dates are documentation dates unless explicitly identified otherwise.
